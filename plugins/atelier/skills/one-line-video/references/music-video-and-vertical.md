# Music video and vertical

## Map the song first
Write a table: section, start, end, bpm, lyric lines with word times, beat grid. Check the tempo is
steady (compare beat position in 10 s windows; drift above about 10 ms [practice] means do not cut to a fixed
grid). Sung word times from automatic transcription drift: verify each line by ear to within 0.2 s [practice].

## One arc, tested
All rules here are [practice].
Give every section one intensity number, 1-5, in the table. Test: the chorus numbers are never
below the verse before them, the last chorus is at least one higher than the first, the bridge is the
lowest after the first chorus. If not, change the shots, not the song.

## Escalation, measured [practice]
Between chorus 1 and the final chorus change at least two of: shot count per bar, scale of the lead,
colour field, camera move, added layer. The same chorus place returns each time; list what changed.

## Lyrics
Hook lines land word by word on the sung syllable; verse lines may run as a strip; never both
for one line. Cuts on bar lines. The key word of a line gets the action (sound hit lands 0 to +1 frame after its picture, never before [up]; check the frame offset).
Deliver a 15-30 s chorus cut for social.

## Vertical (9:16)
Recompose each shot: reposition the subject, then rebuild the type for the narrow frame. Keep
essentials inside x 56-940, y 250-1480 on 1080x1920 (platform bars cover the rest) [up]. Check the face and panel boxes in checks.md for the vertical file.
