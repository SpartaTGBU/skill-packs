# Workflow detail

## Intake: defaults when the brief is silent (all [practice] unless noted)
| Question | Default |
|---|---|
| Where is it seen | social feed, muted autoplay |
| Message | draft it, read back in one sentence |
| Length | 30-45 s story; 15-20 s loop; 8-12 s single vignette |
| Frame | 1080x1920 for vertical feeds; 4:5 or 1:1 for feed posts; 16:9 for web/YouTube |
| Numbers | none on screen without a source |
| Assets | capture real UI; synthesise music from the timeline |

## Missing inputs
- No track: choose tempo and whole bars; generate the score from the timeline.
- No UI: ask once for a screenshot or URL, else dramatise the promise and claim only the brief's words. Abstract type is last resort.
- No product photo/3D: stand-in with no invented detail, say so.
- No voice yet: script with ESTIMATED timings, replace before look proof.
- No fact source: find primary sources and quote with dates, else keep numbers off screen. Numbers, comparisons and "first/best/only" are claims; taglines are not.

## Three words, one default
Turn: where the story changes. Payoff: where meaning lands. Hero: the visual peak. Unless the
brief says otherwise, hero and payoff share the loudest point and the turn sits just before it,
often after a silence.

## Files to leave behind
- TREATMENT.md: idea sentence, hook, terms, chosen structure and why, beat sheet, sound plan.
- FACTS.md: one row per claim with source, date, quote, allowed phrasing.
- timeline.json: global fields (length, fps, size, clock, hero time) plus beats with start, end, text and its px/hold, cue, seam reason, sound, silences.
- Full-size look stills; a delivery report (files, DECISIONs, weakest shot, measured/seen/heard, real vs illustrative numbers).

## Fix card
Scope timecodes | must not change | failure now | observable target | fake fixes to refuse (bigger text, camera move, sharpening, restyle).

## Dry runs
Direct on paper a 12 s brand idle loop, a 30 s changelog recap and a 20 s silent kinetic quote:
name the cue for five reveals, the 3 s hook, and which numbers need FACTS rows.
