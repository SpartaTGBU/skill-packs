# Platform limits and specs

Approximate values as of 2025–2026. Verify before relying on them; platforms change limits often.

| Platform | Post length | Visible before "more" | Hashtags (practical) | Notes |
|----------|------------|----------------------|---------------------|-------|
| LinkedIn | 3,000 chars | ~210 chars | 3–5 | Links in first comment; PDF carousels; articles/newsletters separate |
| X | 280 chars (longer with Premium, ~25,000) | — | 1–2 | Threads; video with captions |
| Instagram | 2,200 chars caption | ~125 chars | up to 5 | Reels and carousels; link in bio or Stories |
| TikTok | ~4,000 chars caption | ~150 chars | up to 5 | Vertical video; hook in first second |
| Facebook | ~63,000 chars | ~125 chars on mobile | 1–2 | Groups; native video |
| YouTube | Title 100, description 5,000 chars | ~100 chars of description | up to 15 (fewer is better) | Chapters; Shorts are vertical and short |
| Pinterest | Title 100, description 500 chars | — | up to 20 (keywords matter more) | Vertical pins |
| Threads | 500 chars | — | 1 topic tag | Conversational |

## Image and video sizes (common defaults)

| Use | Aspect ratio | Typical size |
|-----|-------------|--------------|
| Vertical video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Stories) | 9:16 | 1080×1920 |
| Instagram feed / carousel | 4:5 or 1:1 | 1080×1350 / 1080×1080 |
| LinkedIn / X / Facebook feed image | 1.91:1 or 1:1 | 1200×628 / 1200×1200 |
| LinkedIn document carousel | 4:5 or 1:1 | PDF, one idea per page |
| YouTube thumbnail | 16:9 | 1280×720 |
| Pinterest pin | 2:3 | 1000×1500 |

## Always

- Captions on video (most feed video is watched muted).
- Alt text on images.
- Keep text and faces inside the safe zone; platform UI covers the bottom and right edges of
  vertical video.
- Disclose paid partnerships and affiliate links using the platform's built-in tools and local
  advertising rules.
