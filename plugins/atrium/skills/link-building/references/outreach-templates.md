# Link outreach templates

Starting points only. Rewrite every message for the recipient: name, exact page, exact spot.
Under 100 words. One ask. Sender's real name, role, company; add physical address and an opt-out
line where CAN-SPAM applies. One follow-up maximum.

## Unlinked mention

Subject: Thanks for mentioning <Brand>

Hi <Name>,

Thanks for including <Brand> in "<Article title>". Would you consider linking the mention to
<URL> so readers can find us? Happy to send a better screenshot or a quote if useful.

<Sender>

## Broken link

Subject: Dead link on "<Page title>"

Hi <Name>,

The link to <dead resource> in your "<Page title>" section returns a 404. We published
<Asset> at <URL>, which covers the same ground (<one concrete detail>). It could work as a
replacement, or any other source you prefer.

<Sender>

## List or comparison inclusion

Subject: <Product> for your <list title>

Hi <Name>,

Your "<List title>" is one of the clearer overviews of <category>. <Product> does <one
differentiator relevant to their readers>. If you want to evaluate it, here is free access:
<link or offer>. No obligation to include it.

<Sender>

## Resource page

Subject: Resource for your <topic> page

Hi <Name>,

Your <topic> resources page at <URL> is useful. We built <Asset>, a <what it is> that
<benefit for their audience>. If it fits, it could sit under "<their section>".

<Sender>

## Follow-up (once, 5-7 days later)

Hi <Name>, a quick nudge on the note below. If it is not a fit, no problem and I will not
follow up again.
